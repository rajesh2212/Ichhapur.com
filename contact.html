What a good correction looks like

Four things that move a listing
from “needs confirmation” to listed.

  • Which shopfront, from the road The sign as it reads, and whether the shutters were up. That is what a directory cannot give.
  • Which end of the market it is on A landmark beats a pin. The guide has one owner-supplied point and no verified boundary.
  • A page that already lists it A second source is what lets a listing drop the confirmation badge without anybody walking there.
  • What has changed since it was read Closed, moved, renamed, or never existed. Every source carries the date it was read, so a stale page is visible.

Do not send a telephone number, opening hour or price unless it is written on the shop. The guide publishes what is supplied, and says “Not published” when it is not.
