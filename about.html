ICHHAPUR is a market in Ichhapur, Bhadrak, Odisha. This guide exists because finding out what is actually there meant reading four directories that disagree with each other. So the disagreement is published instead of smoothed over.

What the page does with what it found

Three rules, applied to every listing.

A listing is only as good as the source behind it, and a source is only as good as the date it was read. Where neither is enough, the listing says so on its own face.

    What this guide will not do

    No invented numbers, no invented shops.

    • No telephone number that nobody supplied If the owner has given one it is published; if not, the page says “Not published” rather than guessing.
    • No ratings, reviews or “best in the area” There is no ordering, no payment, no account and no review system, because none of them exist for this market.
    • No photographs of shops that were never photographed Every picture is either a supplied photograph or a drawing of the trade, and the caption says which.
    • No moving the market to a town that fits better The map marker stays on the coordinates supplied by the owner, even though public directories disagree by about 7 km.
    • No claim that a form sends anything The suggestion form is front-end only until a contact channel exists, and the page says so next to the button.
    • No pretending the site is live Nothing is deployed yet — no hosting, domain, database or form service is connected.
