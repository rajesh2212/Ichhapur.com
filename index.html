About the market

A local place, set down plainly

ICHHAPUR is a market area in Bhadrak district of Odisha, a short drive from Bhadrak town, with the postal name Ichhapur and PIN code 756127. This page exists to help visitors get their bearings: what is here, who is here, and how to reach it.

We are deliberately plain about the rest. A market’s history, its size, its busiest days and its specialities belong to the people who shop and trade here — and we have not been given those details yet. When they arrive, they will be added with the source they came from.

What this guide does now is list stores and services, say how far each one is from the market location, show which listings are still shaky, and give you directions.

What this site is

  • A visitor directory of stores and services
  • Search, category filters and directions
  • Source notes on every listing
  • A place to report errors and missing shops

What it is not

  • Not an official register of businesses
  • No online ordering, payments or delivery
  • No ratings or reviews — we have not verified any
  • Not a claim that these are all the shops here
